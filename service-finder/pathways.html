How to use this page

Follow a pathway from left to right.

This page is a visual map. It does not add new content. It translates the existing form logic into a clear route that can be checked before development starts.

1

Choose the pathway

Start with the large title at the top of a card. That is the visitor's selected Step 1 answer.

2

Add the two conditional steps

Build only the Step 2 and Step 3 question shown on that card. The answer options appear underneath each question.

3

Open the final answer

Use the button at the bottom of the card to open the matching final outcome-page reference in the interactive wireframe.

Shared rule

Every visitor completes the same lead-capture Step 4: first name, last name and email are required; phone is optional. Personal may also collect optional suburb if approved.

Build notes

Keep the experience consistent.

What changes by pathway

The selected Step 1 label, Step 2 question and answer, Step 3 question and answer, outcome title, introduction, answer summary, final route and user notification copy.

What stays the same

The four-step form pattern, lead-capture fields, outcome-page layout, three service tiers, What Happens Next content and the relevant audience's service cards.

Personal compliance check

Keep Sudden Change wording neutral and supportive. Place the final approved General Advice Warning directly below the Personal form. Do not use sensitive-event imagery.
