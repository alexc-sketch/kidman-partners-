Shared form rules

How both forms must behave

These rules apply to both the Business and Personal forms. The labels may change, but the experience should stay the same.

Form behaviour

One answer, then continue

Steps 1, 2 and 3 are required image-choice fields. When the visitor selects an option, the form automatically moves to the next relevant question.

Show a Back button on Steps 2 and 3.
Lead capture

Ask for contact details last

Step 4 shows the three selected answers, then asks for first name, last name and email. Phone is optional. Personal may include optional suburb if approved.

Show a clear inline error if a required field is blank.
After submit

Send, save, then redirect

Create the Gravity Forms entry, send the correct user and internal email, then redirect to the relevant outcome page with the submitted answer values.

Every result must show all three chosen answers.

Business Service Finder

Show three Image Choice cards.

The question changes based on the selected Business Stage.

Ask the pathway-specific qualifier

The qualifier adds useful context, such as team size or exit timeline.

Example: Exit sees “What is your intended timeline?”

First name, last name and email are required. Phone is optional. Show selected answers above the fields.

Gravity Forms notifications

Use plain-text emails only

The client has specified that emails cannot use additional styling. Do not add an HTML layout, logo, CSS, colours, images or design workarounds to notifications.

Simple email rule: send one user confirmation that matches the selected Step 1 pathway, then insert the visitor’s Step 2 and Step 3 answers using Gravity Forms merge tags.

3 user emails

  • Start-up Action Plan
  • Growth & Profitability Plan
  • Exit Preparation Plan

  • Retirement Income Plan

Final checks

What “done” looks like

Use this checklist before handing the build back to Kidmans.

✓ Business form has three Step 1 pathways and all conditional questions.
✓ Personal form has four Step 1 pathways and all conditional questions.
✓ All 75 valid combinations can be selected and display the correct three-answer summary.
✓ Step 1 to 3 options auto-advance and Step 2 to 3 include Back controls.
✓ Required lead fields show clear inline error messages if blank.
✓ The Personal disclosure is directly below the form and approved before launch.
✓ Only two reusable outcome layouts are built: one Business and one Personal.
✓ All seven routes show the correct pathway title, introduction and summary copy.
✓ Seven user and two internal notifications are configured in plain text only.
✓ User email includes all three answers, the outcome link, service tiers and next step.

Quick glossary

PathwayThe main choice made in Step 1, such as “Established Business” or “Retiree”.
Conditional logicA rule that shows the correct next question after a user makes a choice.
Merge tagA Gravity Forms placeholder that inserts a saved answer into an email or link.
Outcome templateA reusable page design that changes text and answer values for each user.
RouteThe ending part of the page address, such as /your-plan/exit/.
Plain textEmail content made of text only, with no HTML, design, images or custom styling.
